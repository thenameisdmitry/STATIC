## The Gray Signal

No one has a definitive explanation for the Gray Signal's origins, source, or objective.
Unlike conventional radio waves, it behaves unpredictably and defies the known laws of physics. 
The signal is 
a low-frequency anomalous transmission that requires dense fog to propagate. This phenomenon is
called **Low-Frequency Flickering Echo Effect**.

The Gray Signal adapts to its recipient. Victims encounter various forms of media, 
including late-night television programs, news broadcasts, children's cartoons, 
emergency alerts, and other seemingly ordinary transmissions. Regardless of its appearance, 
every broadcast carries the same purpose: <Redact>to hypnotically compel the recipient to travel 
toward an unknown destination, after which they disappear without a trace</Redact>.

## Operator

The <b>Operator</b> is the name given to the unknown entity, which is responsible for
for transmitting the Gray Signal. Whether the Operator is a living being, an intelligence, 
or something beyond human understanding remains unknown.
