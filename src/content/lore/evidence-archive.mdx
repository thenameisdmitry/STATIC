## Alaska Psychiatric Institute, Anchorage. Patient: Ian Demme, 18 y.o.

<div class="float-right" style="width: 30%">
  <FileFolder label="" cover="/lore/evidence-archive/file-card-3.webp">
    <h4>Initial Psychological Evaluation</h4>
    <p> Patient exhibits severe psychological trauma consistent with acute 
    emotional distress following the recent termination of a long-term romantic 
    relationship. Clinical presentation includes profound depression, emotional 
    withdrawal, survivor's guilt related to the disappearance of five local children, 
    insomnia, and persistent anxiety. </p>
  </FileFolder>
</div>

Following the disappearance of the five children in August 2008, Ian Demme was discovered 
unconscious deep in the woods surrounding Mag Point with a large shard of glass in his left 
forearm. He had suffered severe blood loss and barely survived. Investigators concluded 
that the wound had been self-inflicted and ruled the incident a failed suicide attempt 
brought on by severe psychological trauma (his girlfriend left him abruptly, leaving 
Mag Point for good). With no evidence to suggest otherwise, Ian was committed to the 
*Alaska Psychiatric Institute* for psychiatric evaluation and treatment.

In reality, <Redact>Ian had never intended to take his own life.</Redact> The 
reason behind his actions was an attempt to <Redact>save an abducted child.</Redact>

During his stay at the institution, he encountered another patient, August Pruett, a former
Army signals operator. 
Their meeting would become the first thread connecting Ian to the old mystery of the 
Gray Signal.

## Massacre on Calhoun Avenue

Quote from the police report following the mass shooting on Calhoun Avenue:
> The shooting on Calhoun Avenue occurred in the aftermath of the August 2008 disappearances, 
> when Faraday Kavanagh, the father of one of the missing children, suffered a psychological 
> breakdown after losing hope that his son would ever be found. After taking a search-and-rescue 
> volunteer hostage and demanding the return of his child, he released the hostage unharmed 
> before opening fire on responding police officers from the second floor of his 
> home with a semi-automatic rifle. The attack left three officers dead and three 
> others seriously wounded before Kavanagh was killed during the ensuing police operation.
